A B2B landing page built to convert cold traffic into qualified contacts. One page, five decisions, measurable results.

ClientB2B Agency
GoalB2B lead generation
PlatformWebflow
Result75 leads / week

The brief

Cold traffic from Facebook Ads.
No testimonials. One week to ship.

A B2B agency needed a landing page to convert cold traffic into qualified contacts. The audience knew their marketing wasn't working — they just didn't know who to trust. The page had to speak to that frustration, not pitch a product.

Target audience
SMBs spending on digital marketing with inconsistent results. Business owners, not marketing professionals.
Traffic source
Facebook Ads and organic traffic. Cold audience — most visitors had zero prior brand awareness.
Offer
A free diagnostic guide — low-friction lead magnet that delivers value before asking for commitment.
Constraint
No case study testimonials to use. Social proof had to come from metrics and market presence instead.

5
1
Exit intent popup with a guilt-free offer
When a user moves to close the tab, a popup appears: "Before you go — claim your free guide." The copy mirrors the user's hesitation and the "Prefiero seguir improvisando" button makes saying no feel like the worse choice, without being pushy.
Exit intent Behavioral trigger Lead capture
2
Budget tiers that self-qualify leads
Three investment tiers ($500–800 / $900–1200 / $3000+) anchor the value before asking for contact info and let buyers self-select their budget — so the sales team gets qualified leads, not just curious visitors.
Price anchoring Self-qualification Sales enablement
3
Pain-first copy, not feature-first
"Sistemas rotos. Agencias sin estrategia. Tráfico que no convierte." No preamble, no feature list. The visitor reads their own situation back to them — which creates more engagement than any benefit-focused headline.
Pain copy Empathy trigger B2B framing
4
Metric-based social proof (no testimonials needed)
Without testimonials, the proof came from specific numbers: 4 countries, +40% organic traffic, +44% brand recognition. The specificity (44%, not "nearly 50%") signals real measurement, not marketing estimates.
Social proof Credibility Quantified results
5
Scarcity that's believable
"Solo 5 cupos disponibles este mes" — real scarcity, not a fake countdown. The team can only onboard a limited number of diagnoses per month. Placed right before the final CTA, it gives the visitor one last reason to act now.
Urgency Believable scarcity Pre-CTA placement

The result

A page that worked
from day one.

75
Leads in 7 days
Cold B2B traffic from Facebook Ads converted into qualified contacts with name, email, and budget information.
5
Conversion decisions
Every section on the page had a specific job — no decorative content. Exit intent, pricing, pain copy, proof, scarcity.
B2B
Qualified lead profile
Budget-tier selection means each lead arrives pre-qualified. The sales team knows the prospect's investment range before the first call.
💡
Pain resonates more than features
"Tráfico que no convierte" lands harder than "optimized paid media." Name the frustration the visitor already feels.
⚡
The free offer has to feel worth taking
The lead magnet worked because it offered value before asking for a call. The opt-out button made declining feel like the wrong choice.
📊
Specificity builds credibility
"+44% brand recognition" outperforms "improved brand awareness" — precise numbers imply real measurement, not marketing estimates.
🎯
Let the user tell you their budget
Pricing tiers let prospects self-qualify before the first call. The sales team stopped spending time on unqualified leads.
