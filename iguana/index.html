Iguana PanelDashboardDesign system2026

One panel for
the whole business.

An admin dashboard for service businesses in Ecuador: appointments, clients, money and an AI assistant in one place. Designed mobile-first, on its own small design system, and runnable as a live demo below.

ProductAdmin panel
RoleUX / UI design
LanguageSpanish (EC)
Views6 + dialogs

Context & challenge

Run the day, not the software

Owners of small service businesses (salons, barbers, studios) juggle their agenda, who has paid, what they spent and client messages, often between appointments and from a phone. The challenge: put those jobs in one interface that is fast to read, hard to misread and comfortable with one hand.

Inicio

What matters today

Next appointment with a countdown, money collected and spent today, what is still owed, and the month versus the previous one.

Calendario

Daily timeline first

A week strip plus a day timeline as the main view. Month view is one tap away; on mobile you swipe between days.

Dinero

Income, expenses, profit

Summary, income and expenses, with every change shown as an arrow and a label.

Clientes

Directory and history

Search, history and quick actions to message a client.

Asistente

AI assistant setup

Configure the assistant that answers clients on WhatsApp.

Ajustes

Services, hours, rules

Services, opening hours and booking rules. Invoicing is marked “Soon”.

01 · Empathize

The owner behind the screen

Persona and empathy map are a design synthesis from how this type of business works and from the product’s features. They are working hypotheses to be validated with owner interviews, not research results.

D

Daniela, 35

Owns a small salon with two stylists. Manages everything from her phone.

Goal
Know her day, get paid for every service and see if the month is going well.
Context
Standing next to a client, hands busy, glancing at the phone between appointments.
Fear
Forgetting who still owes her, or double-booking a client.
Habit
Notebook for the agenda, WhatsApp for clients, a notes app for money.

Thinks

  • Who is next, and when?
  • Did she pay the rest?
  • Am I earning more than last month?

Feels

  • Stretched across tools
  • Anxious about missed payments
  • Proud when numbers go up

Says

  • “¿A qué hora es mi próxima cita?”
  • “¿Cuánto me deben?”

Does

  • Checks the phone in 10-second glances
  • Replies to clients between services
  • Reviews money at the end of the day

02 · Define

Pain points in a working day

Four moments of the day, four sources of friction. Each is framed as a question that led to a design decision visible in the demo.

Morning

Scattered agenda

Appointments live in a notebook and in chats, so the day is never one clear list.

How might we…make “my day” one glance?
Between services

No time to read

She has seconds, hands busy, and a small screen.

How might we…show the next appointment and the key number without any tap?
Payment

Partial and pending payments

Deposits and balances are tracked mentally, so debts are forgotten.

How might we…show payment status on every appointment and the total owed?
Clients

Messages interrupt work

Booking questions arrive while she is working.

How might we…answer routine questions for her, and keep WhatsApp one tap away?
End of day

Money in different places

Income, expenses and profit are spread across notes.

How might we…give one place to see how today and this month are going?

04 · Ideate & prototype

UX decisions, grounded in pain points and principles

The chain is the same as in every case: pain point, principle, decision, and how it would be validated. Everything in the first column can be checked in the demo below. Validation is a plan, not a claim of results.

Hierarchy

The daily calendar is the main view

Pain point it answers

The day is the unit of work. A month grid hides what happens next.

UX principleVisual hierarchyMatch with the real world
How to validate

Task: “what is my next appointment?”. Time to answer and errors.

Glanceability

Next-appointment card with countdown and “3/5 appointments today” ring

Pain point it answers

She has seconds, not minutes.

UX principleCognitive loadProgress visualisation
How to validate

5-second test on Inicio.

Clarity

Change versus last period is an arrow plus text, never colour alone

Pain point it answers

Colour-blind users and glare outdoors make colour unreliable.

UX principleRedundant cuesAccessibility
How to validate

Check with colour-blindness simulation.

Payments

Payment status pills on every appointment: Pagado, Pago parcial, Sin pagar

Pain point it answers

Partial payments are easy to forget.

UX principleVisibility of statusRecognition over recall
How to validate

Amount uncollected over time; use of “Cobrar una cita”.

Speed

Quick actions on Inicio: Registrar gasto, Cobrar una cita, Nuevo cliente, Ver calendario

Pain point it answers

The most frequent tasks should be one tap.

UX principleFitts’s lawFlexibility and efficiency
How to validate

Taps to complete each task; frequency of use.

Mobile

Sidebar becomes a bottom tab bar with “Más”; safe areas respected

Pain point it answers

One-handed use on a phone.

UX principleThumb zoneResponsive patterns
How to validate

Tap accuracy; navigation time on a phone.

Navigation

Every view has its own address (#/inicio, #/dinero/resumen)

Pain point it answers

Back button and direct links must work.

UX principleUser controlPredictability
How to validate

Back-button behaviour across views.

Accessibility

Skip link, labelled landmarks, native dialogs, visible focus

Pain point it answers

Keyboard and assistive tech users must reach everything.

UX principleInclusive designStandards
How to validate

Keyboard-only pass; automated accessibility audit.

Prototyping

The interface can be reviewed by opening a single file.

UX principleFast feedback loops

Design system

A small system, defined in tokens

The whole interface is built from a short list of tokens declared once in styles.css: color, shape, a 4/8 spacing scale, two typefaces, layers and layout measures. Components only consume tokens, so a change in one place changes the whole panel.

Typography

Plus Jakarta Sans + JetBrains Mono

Plus Jakarta Sans (400–800) for interface and numbers; JetBrains Mono for times, dates and small labels, which makes schedules scan faster.

Shape & space

Radii 14 · 20 · 28 · pill

Spacing steps 4 · 8 · 12 · 16 · 24 · 32 · 48 px. One icon set with a single 1.75 stroke for the whole panel.

  • Color is never the only signal. Status and change always carry text or an arrow.
  • Semantic colors are reserved. Green = income and success, orange = expense, red = danger. They are not used for decoration.
  • Touch first. Primary controls stay at least 44 px tall on mobile and layouts adapt at tablet, phone and 320–380 px widths.
  • One source of truth. Tokens first, then components, then views.
