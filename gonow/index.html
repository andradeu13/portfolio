01 — Overview

Public transport deserves a better experience.

Every day, thousands of Quito residents navigate the city's public transit with fragmented information, cash payments, and no safety net. GoNow was designed to fix that.

The app brings together route planning, digital payments, and personal safety into one cohesive platform. Every feature traces back directly to something real users told me.

02 — Problem

Three friction points making commuting harder than it has to be.

🚨

Safety

Passengers — especially women and students — described feeling unsafe during trips. No emergency mechanism exists in any transport app for Quito.

🗺️

Route Planning

Finding the right bus combination requires local knowledge or trial and error. No single reliable source of real-time route information exists for the city.

💳

Payment

Cash-only payment slows boarding, increases theft exposure, and creates friction at every single trip. Digital options are fragmented and inconsistent.

03 — Research

“

"I never know if I'll get home safe. I wish there was something I could press if something happens."

— User interview participant · Quito public transit study

Safety was the #1 concern

The most frequently mentioned pain point — incidents of harassment, theft, and zero emergency resources during transit.

Route uncertainty = daily stress

Users often didn't know which bus to take or if a connection would arrive. Every commute included a margin of error they just absorbed.

Payment friction is constant

Cash dependency made passengers vulnerable and slowed boarding. No digital option worked reliably across the full network.

04 — Design Decisions

Four decisions grounded in what users actually told me.

No assumptions. No competitor copying. Each feature traces back to a direct finding from the research.

Decision 01

SOS Safety Button

Safety was the primary unmet need. I designed a persistent, one-tap SOS button accessible from any screen — absent from global competitors like Moovit and Google Maps in this market. It became GoNow's core differentiator.

Decision 02

Unified Navigation Hub

Users needed to move between route lookup, payment, and safety without cognitive overload. A bottom navigation with 4 core sections keeps everything within one tap — wherever the user is in the app.

Decision 03

Simplified Payment Flow

The goal: digital payment in under 3 steps. I mapped the current cash journey and replaced it with a QR-tap system directly accessible from the home screen — removing friction at every boarding point.

Decision 04

Accessible Visual System

Quito's transit users span all ages and literacy levels. I chose a high-contrast palette with clear iconography, prioritizing legibility across demographics — including older adults and low-literacy users.

05 — Process

From interviews to prototype.

A user-centered process — from first conversation to final screen.

01

User Research

Conducted interviews with daily transit users in Quito to identify real pain points — not assumed ones.

02

Problem Definition

Synthesized research into 3 core problem areas: safety, route planning, and payment friction.

03

User Personas

Built personas for primary archetypes — student commuter, working adult, occasional user.

04

Information Architecture

Designed the app structure and navigation hierarchy based on task frequency and user mental models.

05

User Flows

Mapped end-to-end flows for 3 core tasks: plan a route, make a payment, trigger SOS.

06

Wireframes

Low-fidelity screens to validate layout and flow before investing in visual design.

07

High-Fidelity UI

20+ polished screens in Figma with a consistent component system and design tokens.

08

Feedback & Iteration

Prototype reviewed with a design mentor. Iterated based on usability and clarity feedback.
