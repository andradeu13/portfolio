The problem

Every day, humans were
doing what machines should.

The team runs paid campaigns on Facebook and landing pages that generate 80 to 150 leads per week. Before the automation, every single one of those leads went through a three-person chain of completely manual steps. The data person, the ops lead, and the salesperson all touched the same information — copy-pasting, re-entering, and updating things that should have been instant.

The solution

One webhook.
Everything else automatic.

I mapped the full manual flow, identified where a machine could take over, and built a Make (Integromat) pipeline that fires the moment a new lead comes in from Facebook Ads. No CSV. No copy-paste. No re-entry. The lead is automatically created in Monday CRM and logged to Google Sheets in real time, with the dashboard updating itself.

What I delivered

End to end.

01
Workflow mapping & process audit
Documented the full manual chain — every tool, every handoff, every person involved — to identify exactly where automation could replace human effort.
02
Make automation pipeline
Built the Facebook Lead Ads → Webhook → Make flow. The pipeline auto-creates a contact in Monday CRM and logs the entry to Google Sheets the instant a lead submits a form.
03
Real-time Google Sheets dashboard
Designed and built a live reporting layer on top of the automated data feed — showing lead volume by source, pipeline stage, weekly trends, and qualification rates. Always current, no manual refresh.
04
Monday CRM pipeline structure
Restructured the Monday boards so incoming leads are automatically assigned to the right stage and salesperson — giving the sales team a pipeline that organizes itself.
