01
2021

The beginning

I started with screens,
not users.

My first design work was graphic — posters, social media assets, visual identities. I studied multimedia design and audiovisual production at the Instituto Metropolitano de Diseño, and I freelanced from day one. Clients would send a brief, I'd send back something that looked good.

It worked. But something was missing. I was designing for aesthetics, not for people. I was making things look right without asking whether they were the right things to make.

02
2023 – 2024

First real challenges

I felt the gap between
what looks good
and what actually works.

My internship at VSinnova put me in front of medical and tech brands — audiences with real stakes. Then at the Universidad Andina Simón Bolívar, I was designing for an institution that had to communicate clearly across a diverse audience.

Both experiences pushed me to care less about my aesthetic preferences and more about whether the person on the other side understood what they were looking at. That gap — between the designer's intent and the user's reality — started to feel like the actual problem to solve.

Social Media Design Brand Consistency Institutional Design
03
2025

The UX shift

I crossed into UX and
something clicked.

At Autority, I moved properly into UI design — wireframes, prototypes, component libraries, systems thinking. I got my Google UX Design Professional Certificate while doing it. The methodology made sense to me immediately: start with the user, not the screen.

"I wasn't just making things look good anymore. I was figuring out whether they were the right things to build."

That realization changed how I approached every brief. Research before mockups. Users before aesthetics. Problems before solutions.

UI Design Wireframing Prototyping Figma Google UX Cert
04
Oct 2025 – Now

PW Digital

I found a problem no
one had thought to solve.

PW Digital hired me as a UI Designer. Three months later I stepped up to UX & UI Designer, working on conversion-focused experiences and mapping customer journeys for marketing campaigns.

I noticed that every day, people on the team were manually downloading leads, copying them into a spreadsheet, and building the same report from scratch. Three separate steps, every single day. I built a Make pipeline that connected Monday.com to Google Sheets and generated a real-time dashboard automatically.

The workflow was gone. The time came back.

3 steps replaced by one pipeline
1–2h saved per week
Real-time dashboard, zero manual entry
05
6 weeks

Featured project

I wanted to know what it felt
like to build something
from zero.

GoNow was that. A public transit app for Quito — designed from 6 real user interviews over 6 weeks. I mapped pain points, ran competitive analysis, built 20+ screens across 3 design iterations.

One insight from the research stood out: every app in this space buried safety features inside menus. Users mentioned safety concerns in almost every interview. So I put SOS in the tab bar — one tap, always visible, never hidden. Not a feature on the list. A decision driven by what people actually said.
